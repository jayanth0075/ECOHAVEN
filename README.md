# EcoHaven

## About
EcoHaven is a community-driven platform that promotes sustainable living and environmental awareness among young people. It provides a space where users can share eco-friendly tips, participate in sustainability challenges, join community discussions, and track their positive environmental impact. The platform combines learning, collaboration, and action to encourage individuals to adopt more eco-conscious lifestyles.
